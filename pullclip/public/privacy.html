Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

PullClip ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect and how it's used.

Information we collect

PullClip does not require sign-up, accounts, or personal information like your name or email to use the service. When you use PullClip, our server temporarily processes the video link you paste in order to fetch and deliver your download. We do not store your downloaded files — they are automatically deleted from our server shortly after being sent to you.

Like most websites, our server automatically logs basic technical information such as your IP address. This is used only for basic security purposes, such as preventing abuse through rate limiting, and is not sold or shared with third parties.

Cookies and advertising

PullClip displays ads through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about how Google uses this data and manage your ad preferences at Google's Ads Policy page.

Third-party links

PullClip fetches content from third-party platforms (such as Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X, Facebook, and Reddit) based on links you provide. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these platforms.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to download and use any content through PullClip, and for complying with the terms of service of the platform the content comes from.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach us through the contact details on our website.
